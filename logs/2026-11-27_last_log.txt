Endpoint scoring cannot separate an agent that retrieved exactly the evidence a decision required from one that read most of the environment, nor an agent that never located the evidence from one that stopped a single action short. Settling this needs a reference cost, and authored solutions establish only that a task is solvable, not that no shorter route exists. Across 375 scored episodes --- five model endpoints, 25 integrated tasks, three repeats --- success ranges from 58.7% to 96.0% while conditional attainment ranges from 0.803 to 0.890 and orders the models differently.
