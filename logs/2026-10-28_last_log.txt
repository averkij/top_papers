Generated by thinkingmachines/Inkling-Small Financial institutions operating under data-residency constraints are restricted to open-weight models small enough to locally host, which trail frontier systems on multi-step quantitative reasoning. On-policy distillation narrows such gaps by supervising a student on its own rollouts, but traditional OPD requires a strong external teacher, and on-policy self-distillation (OPSD) conditions on a full reference solution. We ask how much of that signal is necessary. We present Fin-SD, a localized hindsight self-distillation recipe for multi-step reasoning.
