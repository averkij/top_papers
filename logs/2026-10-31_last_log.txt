Text:"Multi-Token Prediction Done Right Abstract Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) is now common ingredient in language-model pre-training, as it improves downstream performance and enables faster inference through speculative decoding. We identify flaw in standard MTP training, in which residual component of the MTP gradient interferes with both next-token and multi-token learning, increasingly so with scale. Specifically, the MTP cross-entropy gradient can be decomposed exactly into KL self-distillation term that pulls each MTP head toward the models own next-token prediction (NTP) distribution and improves speculative decoding, and residual that has low signal-to-noise ratio and buries the useful KL signal.
