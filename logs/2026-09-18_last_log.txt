JEPA-Anything 1. Introduction World models learn internal states that allow an agent or scientific model to anticipate unobserved consequences of an observed situation. Early latent world models compressed observations and learned recurrent dynamics for control [12]; more recent systems have shown that learned latent dynamics can support planning across diverse control domains [14]. Across these formulations, the central object is predictive state that retains the information needed to anticipate how the modeled system can change. This perspective extends beyond action-conditioned control. predictive state may summarize physical configuration, patient history, molecular trajectory, partially observed scene, or cellular profile.
